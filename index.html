Test Rules

This site contains test questions related to the history of Kazakhstan. You have 20 seconds to answer each question. If you answer correctly, you will earn 5 points; if your answer is wrong, you will get 0 points.

If you answer a question correctly, you will be allowed to move on to the next question. If your answer is wrong, you will be given two options: play a game or restart.

- If you choose the "play a game" option, a small, fun game will start. If you win the game, you will get a chance to answer the next question, but the question you got wrong will still count as 0 points.
- If you lose the game or skip it, your current score will be displayed, and you will not be able to answer that question again.

If you do not answer within the given time, the question will be counted as wrong, and you will be given the option to play a game or restart. A total of 20 questions are offered in the test.

Game Rules:

You will be presented with 9 boxes. Each box contains different numbers. You need to carefully look at the numbers and memorize them. After memorizing, click the "Hide" button. The numbers in the boxes will then be hidden, and two numbers will be displayed on the screen. Your task is to correctly find and select the displayed numbers from the hidden boxes.
